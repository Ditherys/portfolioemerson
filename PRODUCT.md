# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers at Emerson (Mandaluyong) evaluating a candidate for Senior Supply Chain Analyst. They are reviewing the portfolio to assess whether the candidate can build, present, and explain supply chain analytics in a business context. They are time-pressured and need to form a clear impression quickly. Secondary user: the candidate themselves, using the interview guide to prepare.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio dashboard demonstrating Dither Reyes's supply chain analytics skills through a simulated FY 2024 S&OP performance dataset (Metro Brew F&B Corp.). The deliverable is credibility: the viewer should leave convinced that the candidate can build, interpret, and communicate meaningful supply chain analytics. The dashboard succeeds when a recruiter says "this person knows their stuff."

## Brand Personality

Precise. Credible. Forward-looking.

Voice: senior analyst presenting to a leadership team — confident, concise, no hedging. Not casual, not corporate-stiff. The kind of person who can walk through a heatmap without needing notes.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard templates (blue + white + card grids, Notion-adjacent, Stripe-adjacent)
- AI-generated portfolios with cream/beige backgrounds and editorial display serifs
- Overloaded "look at all my features" dashboards that obscure the signal with noise
- Anything that looks like a free Bootstrap theme

## Design Principles

1. **Data proves the claim.** Every design choice should direct attention to the data, not away from it. Decoration that competes with the chart is wrong.
2. **Precision signals competence.** Tight spacing, consistent alignment, exact values — visual sloppiness reads as analytical sloppiness.
3. **Senior-role confidence.** The interface should feel like it was built by someone who's already done the job — not a student project, not a template fill-in.
4. **Clarity over cleverness.** The recruiter should not have to figure out what they're looking at. Labels, axes, and KPI cards must be immediately legible.
5. **Earned impressiveness.** Wow moments should come from the depth of the analysis (the AI chatbot, the heatmap, the drill-down by product family), not from visual effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as a baseline. The primary audience (recruiters, interviewers) accesses this on desktop during a review session. Mobile should work but is not the primary surface. Reduced motion support required for animated elements.
